How It Works

Step 01

Log in as Staff

Staff account are created through the admin side, to ensure security and integrity who uses the system.

Step 02

Start in timer

In session, table and room timer is shown. Just pick a table or room number and start the timer or set it.

Step 03

Billing

When time is up, the system calculates the exact amount owed based on your configured rates.

Step 04

Payment

Process cash or digital payments directly from the dashboard and print receipts instantly.

Step 05

Report

View real-time occupancy and daily revenue summaries across all tables and rooms.
